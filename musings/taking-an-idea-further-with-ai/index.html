I’ve been experimenting with how AI can help build a product and tell its story. This OneShotLens video is one example.

After I spent a few weeks tweaking UI screens in Claude Design, the Figma connector significantly improved the design and functionality using only natural language. I gave ChatGPT a long-winded prompt about building a real estate finance app and let it run overnight. I was blown away by the results.

To get the details right, I created a database covering 30 deal and transaction types, with skills to generate hypothetical figures and evaluate them against senior credit review standards. I also tried ElevenLabs voiceover. It took a few generations, but worked surprisingly well.

I’m not looking to become a software developer, but I like being able to take an idea this far. Between the UI, the deal scenarios and the voiceover, this gave me a better sense of what I can create with these tools.
