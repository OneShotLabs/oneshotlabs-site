This past weekend, I had the opportunity to return to one of my favorite weekend getaways: Las Vegas.

I’ve spent many weekends in this desert oasis over the years. Fun fact: I met my wife while standing in line at a Starbucks in Las Vegas. True story!

The trip became the catalyst for the short ad above.

The idea was inspired by the familiar line, “Luck is what happens when preparation meets opportunity.”

I’ve been thinking about that idea differently lately.

Those who invest the time to understand and apply new technology today will be best positioned when tomorrow’s opportunities arrive.

Those moments may look like luck to some.

More often, the work that made them possible began long before the opportunity arrived.
