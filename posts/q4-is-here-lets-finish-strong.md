Today is the first business day of the fourth quarter. For a lot of businesses, the next three months will feel like the final minutes of a close game: targets to hit, forecasts to sharpen, budgets to close, and teams working furiously to hit their marks.

The inspiration for this video came from one of my favorite movie speeches, Al Pacino’s “Inches” speech in *Any Given Sunday*. I reimagined it as a call to arms for the year-end rush.

Generative video is becoming a new way to respond to the moment. An idea tied to today’s conversation no longer has to wait weeks for a conventional production cycle. You can see something happening, form a point of view, and turn it into a story while it still matters.

It has also been one of the most challenging creative mediums to master. I stumbled out of the blocks on my first several attempts before developing a system that allows me to move from idea to director’s cut in relatively short order. I’ll share more about that process in time.

The capability is accelerating faster than ever. In 2023, the now-famous AI clip of [Will Smith eating spaghetti](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Will_Smith_Eating_Spaghetti_Original.webm) was a surreal mess of melting faces, hands, and noodles. It became an accidental benchmark. Compare it with what models can produce now, and the improvement is remarkable.

This film is one marker in that evolution and the first in a series.

It has been easy to dismiss AI video to date, and you can still find flaws in this one. But the technology will only become sharper from here. I want to master these production processes now, while the medium is taking off, so I’m well positioned to capitalize on the creative and commercial opportunities that will emerge as the technology continues to mature.
