## The constraint is the point

No zoom means the framing decision happens with your feet, before the shutter — not by cropping later. That's most of what "one shot" means in practice.

## What gets lost, what gets gained

You miss some shots a zoom would have caught. In exchange, the ones you do get tend to be more deliberate. Whether that trade is worth it is the actual argument worth making here, once there's real work to point to.
