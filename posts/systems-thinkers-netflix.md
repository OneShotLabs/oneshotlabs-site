I stumbled across this conversation between Lenny Rachitsky and Elizabeth Stone, Netflix’s Chief Product and Technology Officer, early in my own AI journey. I connected with her message about systems thinkers: people who look across functions, understand how the pieces connect, and identify shared building blocks that help everyone move faster. As more people can build, analyze, and experiment, the challenge becomes creating the right structure around that work.

I especially appreciated her advice to “step out one click.” Take the problem you were handed and zoom out one level. Are you solving the right problem? Could the solution work across more than one team or use case? Are you improving the system, or only completing the task in front of you?

This matters as organizations move AI solutions out of the sandbox and into production. Builders need a framework and a roadmap, with foundational tools that work across disciplines while managing consistency, reliability, and cost.

Many roles draw on the same information, guidelines, regulations, and precedent. Done right, that foundation can be built once, then adapted for role-specific nuance instead of recreated from scratch.

The value of AI is not just speed. It is the opportunity to reconsider the process. What does best-in-class service, user experience, and presentation look like? Design the system to become the standard for how the work gets done, not simply to do the same work faster.
