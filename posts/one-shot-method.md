## Assess

A short, focused audit — a week, not a quarter. The goal is a prioritized list of what's actually worth automating, not a slide deck.

## Build

Whatever gets greenlit gets built against a fixed outcome. No open-ended retainers, no scope that quietly grows every check-in.

## Ship

It goes live, the team gets handed off with what they need to run it, and the engagement ends on purpose — not when the budget runs out.

## Why this instead of a retainer

Retainers optimize for the relationship continuing. This optimizes for the problem getting solved. Both have a place — this is just which one "One Shot" is built around.
